# Reserved strokes come first

[← back to the table of contents](README.md) · [← back to the main README](../README.md)

**Reserved strokes first of all** (`rules/reserved/`), ahead of even briefs: every command,
punctuation mark, and fingerspelled letter in a real keymux config for lyk (copies of the deployed
`01-lyk-commands.json`, `03-lyk-punctuation.json`, `04-lyk-emilys-modifiers.json`) is pinned before
any word is, so a word can never silently take over a stroke like `TP-BG` (`!`) out from under it.
These files are not strokes for words at all; nothing generated competes with them, and no star
form or alternate ever reaches them either.

That's the whole mechanism: this chapter is only about *why* these are pinned first, not what's
actually in them. The content — what strokes, grouped by what they do — has its own chapters once
the rest of the word-generation theory is covered:

- **[Punctuation & commands](punctuation.md)**
- **[Fingerspelling & misc keys](fingerspelling.md)**

Next: **[briefs](briefs.md)** — the next thing pinned down, before the phonetic rules proper.
