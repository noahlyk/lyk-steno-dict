# When two words sound the same

[← back to the table of contents](README.md) · [← back to the main README](../README.md)

**Words that sound the same** are settled in this order. First the **[briefs](briefs.md)**. Then
the spelling decides: `sail` (`ai`) keeps the full vowel `SAEUL`, and `sale` (silent e) takes the
short one `SAEL`, whichever is commoner; the same for `ea` against `ee`, `oa` against `o-e`, and
`air` against `are` — see **[spelling disambiguation](spelling.md)**. Then the commonest word
keeps the plain outline and the rest take a star on the first stroke, a star on the last, then
one and two extra star strokes. A short form never takes an outline that a one-syllable word's
own rules produce.
